The ore bodies we recruit into.

A flotation metallurgist is not a leach metallurgist. A soft rock supervisor is not a hard rock one. We recruit by commodity, method and processing route, and say so when experience does not carry across.

Gold

The Arabian Shield is the Kingdom's gold province. Open pit and underground run side by side, with CIL, CIP and heap leach chosen by ore body.

What clients ask us for:

  • Resource and mine geologists with reportable estimates
  • Grade control supervisors, narrow vein and bulk tonnage
  • Process metallurgists for CIL, CIP and heap leach
  • Underground mine captains and development shift bosses
  • Elution, electrowinning and gold room specialists

Phosphate

Mined in the Northern Borders and processed into fertiliser downstream. Bulk, continuous and heavily mechanised: the workforce looks more like an industrial plant than a hard rock mine.

What clients ask us for:

  • Beneficiation and flotation metallurgists, sedimentary phosphate
  • Large fleet load and haul superintendents
  • Fixed plant maintenance and reliability engineers
  • Bulk materials handling and conveyor specialists
  • Tailings and water stewardship leads

Bauxite & alumina

Al Baitha bauxite feeds the refinery and smelter at Ras Al Khair. Briefs often span mine, refinery and smelter, and the scarce skills sit in the Bayer circuit, not the pit.

What clients ask us for:

  • Bayer process engineers and refinery shift managers
  • Digestion, precipitation and calcination specialists
  • Potline and cast house supervisors
  • Mine planners for bulk, low strip ratio operations
  • Bauxite quality and blending controllers

Copper & zinc

Mostly volcanogenic massive sulphide, mined underground and floated into separate concentrates. Underground hard rock experience is the constraint.

What clients ask us for:

  • Underground mine managers and technical services leads
  • Geotechnical engineers for ground support design
  • Flotation metallurgists for separate Cu / Zn concentrates
  • Ventilation engineers and refrigeration specialists
  • Backfill and paste plant engineers

Industrial minerals

Limestone, silica sand, kaolin, magnesite, gypsum and dimension stone for cement, glass, ceramics and refractories. Lean teams make every hire count.

What clients ask us for:

  • Quarry managers with statutory competencies
  • Product quality and specification engineers
  • Crushing, screening and classification specialists
  • Kiln and calcination operators
  • Multi site operations managers

Downstream & smelting

Smelting, refining and fertiliser at Ras Al Khair and Jubail. The pool overlaps with petrochemicals, so the test is minerals context, not just process background.

What clients ask us for:

  • Smelter and refinery operations managers
  • Process control and automation engineers
  • Furnace, potline and cast house leadership
  • Product metallurgy and quality managers
  • Turnaround and shutdown managers

Rare earths & critical minerals

Early stage and a strategic priority. Hiring is study side and mostly international: separation expertise is still thin in the region.

What clients ask us for:

  • Critical minerals exploration managers
  • Hydrometallurgy and separation specialists
  • Study managers for scoping and prefeasibility
  • Competent Persons for maiden resource estimates
  • Permitting and environmental leads

Aggregates & quarrying

Giga projects have made aggregates a mining business in their own right, run to construction schedules, not mine plans. Hiring is fast and volume led.

What clients ask us for:

  • Quarry and crushing plant managers
  • Drill and blast engineers for construction aggregate
  • Mobile plant and fleet maintenance supervisors
  • Production planners
  • Volume operator and trade intakes

Mining services & contractors

Drilling, mining and earthmoving contractors, laboratories and EPCM firms. Mobilisation speed, contract compliance and commercial sense matter as much as technical depth.

What clients ask us for:

  • Contract managers and project directors
  • Drilling superintendents: RC, diamond and grade control
  • Earthmoving fleet and production managers
  • Laboratory managers and QA/QC leads
  • Commercial managers and quantity surveyors
