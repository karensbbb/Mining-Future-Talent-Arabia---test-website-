Six ways we get people onto site.

Engage us for a single hard-to-fill role or for a full operating roster. Each service runs to its own scope, timeline and commercial model — set out before any work starts.

01 · Retained executive search

The roles that set the operation's trajectory.

A confidential, fully mapped search for leadership seats — and for the boards and investment committees behind them. We build the market map first: every operation in the region and the comparable ones outside it, every person doing the job you are hiring for, and an honest read on who is movable.

Retained search is the right model when the role is scarce, senior or sensitive, when an advert would tip off the market, or when you need the search itself documented for a board or a lender.

TypicallyDetail
RolesGeneral Manager, Mine Manager, Registered Mine Manager, Head of Technical Services, Processing Manager, COO, HSE Director
ModelStaged retainer against agreed milestones
DeliverablesMarket map, longlist rationale, competency-assessed shortlist, referencing pack, offer management
GuaranteeReplacement period set by seniority and agreed up front

02 · Specialist technical hiring

Shortlists built by someone who can read the work.

Discipline-led recruitment for the technical seats that keep an operation honest — resource geology, mine planning, geotechnical, metallurgy, survey and ventilation.

The difference is the screen. A generalist recruiter matches keywords against a job description. We assess against the commodity, the mining method, the plant configuration and the stage of the operation, because a metallurgist who has commissioned a flotation circuit is not interchangeable with one who has run a heap leach.

  • Competency interviews against the actual scope of work, not the title
  • Technical referencing that goes to site performance and decisions made
  • Operating-environment fit — roster, camp, remoteness, family status
  • Certification checks — JORC / NI 43-101 Competent Person status, statutory tickets

03 · Project mobilisation

Roster-scale hiring against a production schedule.

When an operation moves from construction to first ore, hiring stops being a series of vacancies and becomes a logistics problem. We run it as one.

Manning plan

Your roster translated into a hiring schedule — by discipline, by crew, by the week each seat has to be filled.

Volume sourcing

Regional and international campaigns, assessment centres and trade testing, run to the plan rather than to a vacancy list.

Compliance

Medicals, police clearances, trade certification, visa and iqama processing tracked per candidate.

Arrival on site

Flights, camp allocation and induction sequencing so people arrive when the plan says they arrive.
