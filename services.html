Six ways we get people onto site.

One hard to fill role or a full operating roster. Scope, timeline and fees are agreed before work starts.

01 · Retained executive search

The roles that set the operation's trajectory.

Confidential search for leadership seats and the boards behind them. The market map comes first: who does this job today, and who is movable.

Use it when the role is scarce, senior or sensitive, or a board or lender needs the search documented.

TypicallyDetail
RolesGeneral Manager, Mine Manager, Registered Mine Manager, Head of Technical Services, Processing Manager, COO, HSE Director
ModelStaged retainer against agreed milestones
DeliverablesMarket map, longlist rationale, assessed shortlist, references, offer management
GuaranteeReplacement period agreed up front

02 · Specialist technical hiring

Shortlists built by someone who can read the work.

Geology, mine planning, geotechnical, metallurgy, survey and ventilation: the seats that keep an operation honest.

We screen against commodity, method, plant and stage, not keywords. A flotation metallurgist is not a heap leach metallurgist.

  • Competency interviews against the scope, not the title
  • Technical referencing on site performance and decisions
  • Operating environment fit: roster, camp, remoteness, family status
  • Certification checks: JORC and SAMREC Competent Person, statutory tickets

03 · Project mobilisation

Roster scale hiring against a production schedule.

From construction to first ore, hiring stops being vacancies and becomes logistics. We run it that way.

Manning plan

Your roster as a hiring schedule: by discipline, by crew, by week.

Volume sourcing

Regional and international campaigns, assessment centres and trade testing, run to the plan.

Compliance

Medicals, police clearances, trade certificates, visa and iqama, tracked per candidate.

Arrival on site

Flights, camp and induction sequenced so people arrive on the planned day.
