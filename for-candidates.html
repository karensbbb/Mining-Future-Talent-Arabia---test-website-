Candidate questions

Before you register.

Will my current employer find out?

No. Your CV is never sent to a client without your explicit permission for that specific role, we do not approach your current employer, and we do not discuss who is on our books. If there is an operation you specifically do not want approached, tell us and we will block it.

Do you charge candidates anything?

Never. We are paid by the employer. Any recruiter asking a mining candidate for a placement fee, a visa fee or a processing fee is not operating legitimately — that applies to us and to everyone else.

I have no Middle East experience. Is that a problem?

Usually not. A large share of the senior technical population on Gulf operations came from Australia, South Africa, Canada, Chile and the Nordics without regional experience first. What matters far more is whether your commodity, mining method and operating stage line up with the brief.

What are the rosters actually like?

It varies widely by operation and by role. Residential family-status packages are common on the larger integrated operations; remote exploration and construction work is more often rotational. We will tell you the specific roster, camp standard and flight routing for any role before you interview, because that is what decides whether a hire lasts.

I am a Saudi national. Do you work with local candidates?

Yes, and it is a growing part of the desk. Nationalisation targets mean operators are actively building Saudi technical and leadership capability, and Saudi professionals with international mining experience are among the most sought-after people in the market. We also run graduate and apprentice pipelines with operators.

How long until I hear back?

A consultant reviews every registration. If your discipline matches something live, you will hear quickly. If it does not, you will still hear — we would rather tell you there is nothing right now than leave you waiting on a silence.
