The mandate

Ore bodies are the easy part. People are the constraint.

The geology and the capital are in place. What every operator, contractor and investor is competing for is proven mining talent on the ground.

Your competitors are mobilising now. The people who can run the plant will join their roster or yours.

Employed.

The people you need are already in a job and will not answer an advert. That is a search problem, not an advertising problem, and it is the one we are built for.

Mining is now Saudi Arabia's third industrial pillar. Oman is restarting copper, the northern Emirates quarry at industrial scale, and Gulf investors are buying into African copper, cobalt, tin and gold. Every one of those projects needs the same scarce people.

The shortage is specific. CVs are plentiful. Resource geologists who have signed off an estimate, metallurgists who have commissioned a flotation circuit and supervisors who hold a safety standard through a ramp up are not.

We recruit against competency and operating environment, not job titles, with each country's nationalisation rules built in from day one.

Common questions

Before you send the brief.

Do you only recruit in Saudi Arabia?

No. We search across Saudi Arabia, the UAE (including Fujairah and Ras Al Khaimah), Oman and Africa, from Copperbelt copper and cobalt to West African gold.

We source globally: your next geologist may come from Perth, Johannesburg or Santiago.

How do you charge?

Retained search runs on a staged retainer. Volume and contract hiring are priced per placement or as a margin on the rate.

You see the terms in writing before work starts, and the market view is free.

What happens if a placement does not work out?

Permanent placements carry a replacement guarantee, agreed up front. If a hire leaves inside it, we search again at no extra fee.

Can you help us hit nationalisation targets without losing capability?

Yes. We treat it as workforce planning, not a quota: national pipelines built alongside expatriate hires, with named successors and handover dates in the mandate.

We are not yet in production. Is it too early to talk?

It is usually too late, not too early. A General Manager search can take six months from first call to site.

I am a candidate. Will you keep my details confidential?

Yes. Your CV goes nowhere without your permission for that specific role, and we never approach your employer.
